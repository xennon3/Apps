Driving lock

Covers the car screen and pauses games while the car is moving.

Turn back on every drive

Each new connection to the car starts with the driving lock on.

First time in the car?
  1. Open Settings → Apps → Android Auto on this phone and go to the Android Auto settings.
  2. Tap Version at the bottom ten times to unlock developer mode.
  3. In the ⋮ menu choose Developer settings and turn on Unknown sources.
  4. Reconnect to the car. Dashplay is in the Android Auto app launcher.

Turn off the driving lock?

Only do this when a passenger is using the screen. The driver should never play or browse while driving.
